Slices can now follow the data: a taint-path slice keeps the source, the sink, the statements that define what they read and every guard that controls them (an early `raise` or `return` included) and drops the rest of the function; a backward slice does the same from the sink alone, a forward slice from a source, a sink window from a location, and a hotspot slice marks code for the discovery task. `privacy.slice.defuse_depth` and `privacy.slice.callee_depth` bound how far a slice follows definitions and function calls, and `default_strategy` accepts `sink_window`. A slice records which strategy built it, why a richer one was skipped, and how much of the path it kept.

The vault now has a lifecycle. `vault_info` reports its format, key source, record and scan counts, rotation history and whether its key is reachable without decrypting a record; `rotate_key` moves the data key to a new master key (or also replaces the data key and re-encrypts every record) with a crash-safe rename; `destroy` removes the vault and its key after a confirmation token; and `privacy.vault.retention_days` deletes the mappings of old scans at the start of a scan. A new `kms` key source (`privacy.vault.kms.*`) keeps the master key in a HashiCorp-Vault-transit-compatible service reached over TLS through an allow-listed host; it is off by default, refused in the `airgapped` profile, and recorded as an exception to the single-egress decision in `docs/adr/ADR-0003-exception-kms.md`.

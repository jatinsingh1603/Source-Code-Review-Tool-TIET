`codekavach doctor` also checks what the settings ask for: one `engine:<name>` check per external engine, `provider:<id>:configured` and `provider:<id>:reachable` per enabled provider, and `report:pdf` and `report:fonts` when PDF reports are requested. A missing engine, key or library is a warning. The provider probe is opt-in (`--probe-providers`), goes through the egress transport, follows the consent gate of a scan (`--accept-egress`), is skipped under `--offline` for a remote provider, and `doctor` never asks the consent question or stores a grant. The checks are listed in `docs/reference/cli-doctor.md`.

Dependency inventory and supply-chain scanning: manifests and lock files for Python, JavaScript, Java, Go, Ruby, PHP and Rust become a CycloneDX 1.5 or SPDX 2.3 bill of materials (`codekavach sbom`), vulnerable pinned dependencies are matched against an offline OSV mirror with KEV and EPSS enrichment and a reachability hint, and `codekavach sca update` is the one explicit, allow-listed network action that refreshes the mirror (refused with `--offline`).

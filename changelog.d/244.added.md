Provider conformance suite: `pytest tests/conformance -m conformance` checks every registered adapter offline, replays a response recorded from AgentRouter (OpenAI Responses) against the bundled synthetic payload, and runs a live probe only with `CODEKAVACH_CONFORMANCE_LIVE=1`. `tools/record_conformance.py` records new cassettes for allow-listed synthetic payloads only, with no header or account identifier. The built-in `agentrouter` provider is always listed by `codekavach providers list` and also takes its key from a file named by `CODEKAVACH_AGENTROUTER_API_KEY_FILE`.

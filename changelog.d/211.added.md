The mapping vault: `Vault` stores pseudonym-to-original mappings in a local SQLite file under AES-256-GCM envelope encryption (a random data key sealed by a master key from the OS keyring, a scrypt passphrase or, for tests, an ephemeral key; the key-management source arrives with E10-03). Records are bound to their scan and pseudonym, nonces come from a persisted counter, and the vault gives `lookup`, `reverse`, a lazy `iter_originals` for the egress guard and a `get_or_create_salt` for the persistent per-project scan salt. The new `privacy.vault.retention_days` setting (restricted, not enforced yet) and the `vault-record-locality` import contract (only the vault and the restore engine may import `MappingRecord`) go with it.

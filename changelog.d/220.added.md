Egress ledger writer, reader and payload store: every request that leaves, or is blocked before it leaves, is appended as a sealed entry in a hash-chained file under the state directory, with its sanitised payload stored once per distinct hash. Payloads are encrypted at rest under a data key wrapped by the vault's key sources (`privacy.egress.encrypt_at_rest`, on by default), and the chain is verified entry by entry together with the payloads, reporting the first divergence by sequence number. The chain is tamper-evident against modification, insertion, deletion, reordering and truncation, but not against a complete rewrite by someone who can recompute every hash, because nothing anchors it outside the state directory. Pruning removes old payload bodies only, and leaves a tombstone that reads as pruned rather than as corrupt.

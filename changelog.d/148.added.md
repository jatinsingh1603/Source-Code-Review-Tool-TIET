A scan now keeps the cache within `scan.cache_max_size_mb` and keeps the records of the `scan.cache_keep_scans` newest scans: after the scan, least recently used blobs and memo entries are deleted, old scan records and stale temporary files are removed, and stage records that point at a deleted blob are dropped. Pruning is best effort and never fails a scan. Deleting is ordinary unlinking, not secure erasure.

The privacy layer gains the redaction framework: a `Detector` protocol and `DetectorRegistry` (plugins join through the `codekavach.detectors` entry-point group), `TypedSpan` with a closed subtype vocabulary, deterministic overlap resolution, `detect_all` with a counts-only report, `mask_placeholders` so repeated redaction changes nothing, `safe_compile` to reject patterns that can backtrack catastrophically, and a `Redactor` that writes typed placeholders such as `<SECRET:aws_access_key:1>` with a value-keyed, salted `PlaceholderNumbering`. Redaction fails closed: any problem raises `RedactionError` instead of returning partial text. Two restricted settings are added: `privacy.pii_locales` and `privacy.detect.min_confidence` (default `low`, so recall wins over precision).

Personal-data and domain-term redaction detectors: Indian formats (Aadhaar with the Verhoeff check, PAN, IFSC, UPI, Indian mobile, GSTIN) and international ones (email, E.164 phone, payment card with Luhn and issuer prefix, IBAN with mod 97, IPv4, IPv6, MAC address, date of birth, and an opt-in passport number), enabled by `privacy.pii_locales` (an unknown locale is now a configuration error), plus a dictionary-driven domain-term detector built on an Aho-Corasick automaton that matches case-insensitively across `-`, `_`, `.`, white space and camel case. Documented test cards are reported at low confidence instead of dropped.

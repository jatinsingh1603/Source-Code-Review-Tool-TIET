Native rule format and loader: `codekavach.analysis.rules` now has the rule models (`RuleDef`, `MatchBlock`, `PatternClause`, `Constraint`, `TaintSpec`, `RuleMeta`), rule packs with a SHA-256 checksum over the sorted rules, a safe YAML loader (strict UTF-8, 1 MiB cap, no symlinks, no anchors, aliases, tags or deep nesting, regular expressions compiled at load, nothing executed) and fourteen `CK-RULE-nnn` validation codes, including an unsafe-regular-expression check. `codekavach rules list|show|validate|bundle` reads packs; `bundle` writes a deterministic `.ckrules` archive that loads offline. `engines.native.rules = false` loads nothing. The JSON Schemas are in `docs/schemas/rules/` and the field reference in `docs/reference/rule-metadata.md`, both generated by `python -m codekavach.analysis.rules.schema`. `rules/mit/` holds a one-rule seed pack. PyYAML becomes a runtime dependency.

The pseudonymiser gains its generator: `PseudonymGenerator` turns an internal name into a kind-preserving token such as `fn_1` or `var_3`. The HMAC of the scan salt and the name decides the order in which a kind's names are numbered, so the numbering depends on the salt and the set of names and not on the slice, and numbers are dense. `MappingBatch` carries the pairs to the vault and shows counts only in a `repr`. The new `privacy.pseudonym.link_mode` (`per_scan` or `per_request`) and `privacy.pseudonym.role_hints` (restricted, off) make linkability and role hints settings.

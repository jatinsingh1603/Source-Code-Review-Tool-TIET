`codekavach plugins` (and `plugins list`) lists every installed plugin with its status (`ok`, `error`, `shadowed` or `disabled`), and `codekavach plugins check` reports whether the installed stages resolve into a valid order: the order, plugins that failed to load, name collisions, unmet requirements, duplicate providers and a cycle. `check` exits 1 when the order does not resolve or a plugin failed to load; `--strict` also fails on collisions. A failed plugin is shown by its entry-point coordinates and the class name of its exception, never its message, with or without `--debug`. `codekavach doctor` gains the optional check `plugins:pipeline`.

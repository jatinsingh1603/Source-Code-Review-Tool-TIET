`codekavach scan` now rates its candidates into findings: severity from the CVSS score, the engine or the CWE family, evidence cut from the original files, and the model's restored answer recorded in each finding's provenance. A model that calls a candidate not vulnerable lowers its confidence but never removes it.

`codekavach report` now renders a self-contained HTML report (and a PDF when the optional `reports` extra and the Pango libraries are installed): summary, severity-by-likelihood matrix, findings with numbered text snippets, and a privacy attestation. Model and client text is escaped, the page loads nothing remote and carries a content-security policy.
Inspect pages now rebuild payloads from a persisted stub plus the ledger text.

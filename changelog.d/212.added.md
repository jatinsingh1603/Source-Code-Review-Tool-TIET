Restore stage and restore engine: the model's verdict is turned back into the client's terms before anyone reads it. Pseudonyms are replaced by their originals through one batched vault query per verdict, and placeholders become plain phrases (a secret is named by its kind and never shown). Cited payload lines are mapped through the slice to real file lines, and a line that maps nowhere is dropped. A claim that cites an unknown name, or a verdict whose primary assertion cannot be anchored, is dropped and counted, so the candidate keeps its deterministic evidence. A malformed verdict is reported as a failed item and the rest of the scan is restored. New stage `restore` (category restore, fail closed) registered under `codekavach.stages`; the vault gains `lookup_many`.
